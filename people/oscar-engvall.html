Oscar Engvall

Bass-baritone, performing the Father

Oscar Engvall is a bass-baritone from Uppsala and a first-year student in the bachelor's programme in opera at Stockholm University of the Arts. He began his vocal studies in 2023 with two years at Birkagården Folk High School.

He also holds a bachelor's degree in literature and musicology from Uppsala University. Last autumn, Oscar received the Ulf Törnqvist and Terje Groe Langseth scholarship for young singers.
