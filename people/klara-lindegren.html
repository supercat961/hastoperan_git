Klara Lindegren

Soprano, performing Jules

Klara Lindegren (born 2002) is a Swedish soprano trained at SKH Opera, formerly the University College of Opera in Stockholm. She made her debut at the Royal Swedish Opera in spring 2025 as Barbarina in The Marriage of Figaro, conducted by Alan Gilbert and directed by Linus Fellbom, and returned to the role in autumn 2025 under the musical direction of Tobias Ringborg.

During the 2025/26 season, she also appeared in the chorus of Folkoperan's Tosca and in newly written music theatre in A Midsummer Night's Dream at Orionteatern. She is currently performing in Årsbanketten, an SKH Performing Arts project presented in the Golden Foyer of the Royal Swedish Opera. In the autumn, she will begin master's studies at the Royal College of Music in Stockholm.
