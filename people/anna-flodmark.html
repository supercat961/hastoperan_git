Anna Flodmark

Pianist

Anna Flodmark is a versatile pianist and performer who explores the boundaries between different art forms, genres and modes of expression. She has a particular interest in contemporary music and often collaborates with composers to perform and premiere new works.

She also has a great passion for collaborating with singers and performing in a variety of chamber music settings. Anna studied with Jens Elvekjær at the Royal Danish Academy of Music in Copenhagen, with Marianna Shirinyan at the Norwegian Academy of Music in Oslo, and with Johan Fröst at Lilla Akademien in Stockholm.

She currently works as a freelance musician across the Nordic region and is also chair of Ung Nordisk Musik Sweden.
