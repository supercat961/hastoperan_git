Anna Nygren

Librettist

Anna Nygren (born 1990) is an author, playwright, literary scholar and artist. She is a senior lecturer in literary composition at HDK-Valand.

Anna has written for independent theatre companies including Barnteaterakademin in Gothenburg and The Play Group in Cape Town, and has worked as a dramaturg at institutions including Regionteater Väst. Her debut novel, Hunger, was published in 2020, and she has also written the young adult book Fuck Your Marsvin And Dö.
