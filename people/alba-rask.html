Alba Rask

Assistant lighting designer

Alba Rask is in the first year of the bachelor's programme in Performing Arts, specialising in lighting design, at Stockholm University of the Arts.

She has previously worked as a lighting technician at the Royal Dramatic Theatre, Dansens Hus, Riksteatern, Dansnät Sverige, Lumination, MDT and Norbergfestival. Her lighting design credits include Dunkel, Groove, Raised on Rhythm, Phakathi Inside and Drag Light District.

Alongside her studies, she works at the Royal Dramatic Theatre and freelances. She grew up in Luleå and is now based and active in Stockholm. She discovered her love of light through dance and raves.
