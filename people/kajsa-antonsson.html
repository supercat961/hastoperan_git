Kajsa Antonsson

Production producer

Kajsa Antonsson is a Malmö-based producer working in performing arts and experimental music. She has produced numerous performance works, concerts and festivals, and has worked with artists and organisations including Khamlane Halsackda, Tuva Hildebrand, Poste Restante, Danscentrum Syd, Sound of Stockholm, Nordic Music Days, Föreningen Levande Musik and Konstmusiksystrar.

Kajsa holds a degree in international cultural project management from Kulturverkstan and has an artistic background as a composer.
