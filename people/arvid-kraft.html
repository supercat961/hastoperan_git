Arvid Kraft

Composer & dramaturg

Arvid Kraft (born 1997) is a composer, musician, poet and organiser whose work focuses on the interplay between meaning and abstraction in music. His works, often incorporating elements of performance, have been presented at festivals such as Sound of Stockholm and internationally at Nordic Music Days.

Arvid is a member of the group Kid08, treasurer and producer for the Swedish Foundation for Young Nordic Music, and a producer for Echo & Altar Records.
