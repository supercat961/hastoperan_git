Alica Tserkovnaja

Director, choreographer & dramaturg

Alica Tserkovnaja (born 1988) holds a bachelor's degree in mime acting from Stockholm University of the Arts and is also a classically trained flautist, educated at the Malmö Academy of Music. She currently works as an interdisciplinary artist across choreography, directing and sound art.

Her work has been presented at Nobel Week Lights, Inter Arts Center and Konträr in Stockholm, among others. Alica serves on the board of Fylkingen and works as a theatre educator at Kulturskolan Stockholm.
