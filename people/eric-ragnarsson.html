Eric Ragnarsson

Site manager / assistant producer

With a keen eye for detail and a strong sense of the whole, Eric has broad experience in the Swedish performing arts, in roles ranging from stage technician to producer.

Eric began his career at Värmlandsteatern as a lighting technician, production assistant and producer, before continuing at Wermland Opera in several technical and production-related roles. He has also worked at the Royal Swedish Opera as a production assistant in stage technology and as technical coordinator for Stockholm 59° North.

Today, Eric works as stage manager and assistant stage manager at Confidencen. His technical expertise, creative drive and solution-oriented approach make him a dependable and committed collaborator in every production.

Eric graduated as a technical production coordinator from Stockholm University of the Arts in spring 2026.
