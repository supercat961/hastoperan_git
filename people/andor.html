Andor Söderberg

Boy soprano, performing Jackie as a child

Born in 2013, Andor has sung with the Stockholm Boys' Choir for five years. In 2025, he was named Boy Soprano of the Year in the national competition in Rättvik. He has appeared as a soloist in Lloyd Webber's Requiem, Fauré's Requiem, and Nyberg's folk mass Himmelen inom. Outside music, Andor is an enthusiastic scout, sailor and angler.
