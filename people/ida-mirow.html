Ida Mirow

Dramaturg

Ida Mirow works across literature, stage and film as a writer, director and dramaturg. Her debut book, the poetry collection Den mjukaste vännen, was published by It-lit in 2022. She founded Pralin Magasin, which she has run since 2018, and has also taught and mentored creative writing.

In autumn 2026, she is the dramaturg for Hästoperan and the playwright and director of Döden är inget utan dig at Teater Brunnsgatan Fyra.

Her education includes a master's degree in Performance Practice as Research from the Royal Central School of Speech and Drama in London.
