Adele Kosman

Sound engineer

Adele Marcia Kosman (born 1993) is a composer, vocalist and developer, trained in electroacoustic composition at the Royal College of Music in Stockholm.

Her practice moves between vocal works, sound installations, acousmatic composition and live performance. Alongside her solo work, she fronts the alternative rock band Kid08 together with composer Arvid Kraft.
